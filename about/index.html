About

Welcome to Liberty Gardens Estate

Your Pathway to Exceptional Homes

Liberty Gardens Estate was founded to provide a lifestyle that combines modern housing, security, and community living in the heart of Prampram, Ghana. We are more than just real estate — we’re creating sustainable communities where families can live, grow, and enjoy peace of mind.

With a team of experienced architects, builders, and planners, we focus on delivering high-quality gated homes and serviced plots designed with elegance, durability, and convenience in mind.

Our estate features world-class infrastructure, reliable utilities, serene green spaces, and a supportive community — making it the ideal home for Ghanaian families and investors alike.

Our Journey

For over a decade, Liberty Gardens Estate has embarked on an incredible journey, transforming the landscape of Ghana with our iconic developments. Our vision to create exceptional living spaces has driven us to push boundaries, explore new horizons, and set new benchmarks in the real estate sector.

Commitment to Quality

At Liberty Gardens Estate, quality is the cornerstone of everything we do. We take great pride in our meticulous attention to detail, superior craftsmanship, and the use of premium materials to deliver homes that are not only beautiful but built to stand the test of time.

Our Philosophy

At Liberty Gardens Estate, we believe a home is more than bricks and mortar—it’s a reflection of dreams and memories. Guided by a philosophy that puts the customer first, our dedicated team works to create living spaces that enrich lives and nurture communities.

Trusted Developer for Peaceful Living in Ghana

Liberty Gardens Estate is a trusted brand dedicated to helping people settle in serene, less noisy environments. We create comfortable communities that offer a true sense of freedom — where modern living meets calm surroundings, just moments away from the refreshing breeze of the beach.

Join the LGE Family

As we continue to forge ahead, we invite you to embark on a journey of extraordinary living with Liberty Gardens Estate. Whether you seek a serene family abode, an investment opportunity, plan units, we have the perfect plan for you.

Thank you for considering Liberty Gardens Estate. Together, let’s create a future of unparalleled possibilities in the realm of real estate.
