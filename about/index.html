Our Process

Register your interest by clicking the button above. We’ll reach out to understand your needs and provide detailed guidance.

Select your preferred home (3 or 4 bedroom). Pay a non-refundable reservation fee of GHS 50,000 which contributes to the home’s price.

Receive a Sales and Purchase Agreement. Submit it within 30 days or the property will be returned to the market.

Pay 30% of the house price (minus reservation fee) within 30 days — building commences.

At the shell stage, 50% of the house price is required within 30 days.

During internal fittings and finishes, pay 10% of the house price within 30 days of notification.

Completion stage — the final 10% payment is made before handover.

Receive all legal documents and keys to your new Liberty Gardens Estate home.
