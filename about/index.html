About

Welcome to Liberty Gardens Estate

Your Pathway to Exceptional Homes

Liberty Gardens Estate was founded to provide a lifestyle that combines modern housing, security, and community living in the heart of Prampram, Ghana. We are more than just real estate — we’re creating sustainable communities where families can live, grow, and enjoy peace of mind.

With a team of experienced architects, builders, and planners, we focus on delivering high-quality gated homes and serviced plots designed with elegance, durability, and convenience in mind.

Our estate features world-class infrastructure, reliable utilities, serene green spaces, and a supportive community — making it the ideal home for people who want to choose Ghana as their settlement and investors alike.

Our Process

Step 1

Register your interest by clicking the button above. We’ll reach out to understand your needs and provide detailed guidance.

Step 2

Select your preferred home (3 or 4 bedroom). Pay a non-refundable reservation fee of GHS 50,000 which contributes to the home’s price.

Step 3

Receive a Sales and Purchase Agreement. Submit it within 30 days or the property will be returned to the market.

Step 4

Pay 30% of the house price (minus reservation fee) within 30 days — building commences.

Step 5

At the shell stage, 50% of the house price is required within 30 days.

Step 6

During internal fittings and finishes, pay 10% of the house price within 30 days of notification.

Step 7

Completion stage — the final 10% payment is made before handover.

Step 8

Receive all legal documents and keys to your new Liberty Gardens Estate home.
