Why Choose Liberty Gardens Estate?

Why Prampram?

Prampram offers a serene and tranquil environment, free from the noise and air pollution of urban life.

Its pristine coastline and peaceful atmosphere make it ideal for both residents and investors seeking a refreshing lifestyle.

With proximity to Tema and numerous free zone companies, Prampram presents immense economic opportunities.

The ongoing expansion of the Tema–Prampram road into a 6-lane highway will make the area even more accessible — less than 20 minutes’ drive from Tema.
